# TrueCost

> Your budget, watching your back while you shop.

TrueCost is a Chrome extension for [Household](https://amsterhamster.com) users. It shows your real budget impact the moment you're about to buy something on Amazon — before you click "Add to cart."

---

## Why TrueCost?

You set budgets in Household. But by the time you open the app, the impulse purchase has already happened.

TrueCost bridges that gap — it's present at the exact moment of decision, right on the Amazon product page.

- 🟢 **Badge on your toolbar** — always know where you stand with your budget
- 💸 **Budget impact** — see exactly what % of your budget this purchase uses
- ⏱️ **Hours of work** — understand the real cost ("this costs 4.5 hrs of your work")
- 🧠 **Need / Want / Indulge** — classify the purchase and get a cooling-off nudge for impulse buys
- 🏠 **Personal & Household** — toggle between your personal and shared household budget

---

## Installation

TrueCost is available for Household users only. You'll need an existing Household account to use it.

### Step 1 — Download

👉 **[Download TrueCost v1.0.0](https://github.com/brewster-web/truecost/releases/latest/download/truecost-extension.zip)**

### Step 2 — Unzip

Unzip the downloaded file to a folder on your computer. Remember where you put it.

### Step 3 — Load in Chrome

1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`
2. Enable **Developer mode** using the toggle in the top right
3. Click **Load unpacked**
4. Select the unzipped `truecost-extension` folder
5. The TrueCost icon will appear in your Chrome toolbar

### Step 4 — Sign in

1. Click the TrueCost icon in your toolbar
2. Sign in with your **Household** username and password
3. That's it — your budget is now watching your back

---

## How it works

Once installed, visit any product page on **Amazon.in** and a nudge banner will appear showing:
- The product price
- Your budget usage after this purchase
- How many hours of work this purchase costs
- Need / Want / Indulge classification buttons

Click the extension icon anytime to see your monthly budget summary.
